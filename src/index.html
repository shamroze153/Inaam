  2. 02 / Goondhna

    Haath se goondha, sabr se rakha.

    Aata tab tak goondha jata hai jab tak resham jaisa naram na ho jaye. Phir usay araam diya jata hai.

  3. 03 / Belna aur nishaan

    Gol bela, kaante se nishaan.

    Yeh nanhe nishaan sheermaal ki pehchaan hain. In ki wajah se roti tandoor mein ghubbara nahi banti, barabar pakti hai.

  4. 04 / Tandoor

    Mitti ke tandoor ki aag.

    Garam tandoor ki deewar par chand minton mein rang doodhiya se sunehra ho jata hai.

  5. 05 / Zafrani lep

    Zafrani ghee ka lep. Inaam tayyar.

    Tandoor se nikalte hi upar zafran aur ghee ka lep, jo isay woh mashhoor chamak deta hai.
